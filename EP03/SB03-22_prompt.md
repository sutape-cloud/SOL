GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Nine shots, 27 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: small and natural. Feelings show quietly. The only light is the morning sun and its reflections off the glass front of the building and the white car. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain, the car and the motorcycle included: plain grille, plain wheel centres, blank number plates. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. Each shot keeps one fixed focal length. Whenever Aoy smiles, it is a closed-lip smile. The only sound is the real sound of the place.

LIP SYNC
Juk speaks in Shots 3, 6 and 8; Aoy speaks in Shots 4, 7 and 9. Each line is lip-synced to the person saying it. The voice files play in order, each exactly once: @Audio1 (Juk) from 6.3 s, @Audio2 (Aoy) from 8.6 s, @Audio3 (Juk) from 13.2 s, @Audio4 (Aoy) from 14.3 s, @Audio5 (Juk) from 19.1 s, @Audio6 (Aoy) from 22.9 s. A voice file may end with a stretch of silence that is there only to make the file long enough; only the spoken words are used, and the acting carries straight on to the next beat. Everything ends within 27 seconds.

BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. Inside the lobby, Aoy saw Juk's white car pull up and told Win to hide; Win ran deep into the building. Juk has come to take her to work. Yesterday, Aoy's "grandmother" told Juk that Aoy's uncle was so ill he might not live through the night. Now Juk sits behind the wheel of his car at the kerb right in front of the glass door, and Aoy is pushing the glass door open to go out and stop him at the door.

SCENE
Aoy must keep Juk out of the building, because Win is hiding inside. She plants herself in front of the glass door and refuses to go to work: she has to look after her uncle, just out of hospital. Juk smells a lie and throws yesterday back at her: wasn't this the uncle who was not supposed to last the night? Aoy, straight-faced, claims her uncle got better the moment he saw her face, so much that the doctors were amazed, and she is so carried away by her own story that she stands there for a second, eyes closed; in that second Juk slips past her and walks into the lobby. Aoy is stubborn on the outside and panicking inside. Juk is all sugar on the surface and hard underneath.

AOY'S FACE
Her skin tone is natural, as in her character references. Her stubbornness and her panic inside show in her darting look while she makes things up.
