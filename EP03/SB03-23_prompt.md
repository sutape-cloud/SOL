GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Six shots, 26 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: small and natural. Feelings show quietly. The only light is the morning sun coming through the glass front wall, the warm hidden lights along the edge of the ceiling, the downlights, and the brass wall lamps; in the housekeeper's rest room, only the small window and the small dome light on the ceiling. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain, the car included: its grille and wheel centres are plain and its number plates are blank. The lift door and its panel are plain brushed steel with only a plain call button. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. Each shot keeps one fixed focal length. Whenever Aoy smiles, it is a closed-lip smile. The only sound is the real sound of the place.

LIP SYNC
Juk speaks in Shots 1 and 4; Aoy speaks in Shots 3 and 6. Each line is lip-synced to the person saying it. In Shot 2 the end of Juk's first line comes faintly through the gap of the wooden door while the picture stays on Win in the rest room. The voice files play in order, each exactly once: @Audio1 (Juk) from 0.3 s, @Audio2 (Aoy) from 9.8 s, @Audio3 (Juk) from 12.0 s, @Audio4 (Aoy) from 21.3 s. A voice file may end with a stretch of silence that is there only to make the file long enough; only the spoken words are used, and the acting carries straight on to the next beat. Everything ends within 26 seconds.

BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. Outside the building, Aoy blocked the glass door and swore she had to stay home to look after her sick uncle; while she stood there, carried away by her story of the amazed doctors, Juk slipped past her, pulled the glass door open and stepped inside. Win, who is the "uncle", ran deep into the corridor to the lift and hid behind the wooden door at its far end, which turned out to be the housekeeper's rest room. He brought the contract folder with him and put it on the table there.

SCENE
Juk is sure the sick uncle is made up, and he walks in to test it: if the uncle is upstairs, he will go up and say hello. He acts the perfect polite boyfriend, but every step is a push into Aoy's place. Aoy runs after him, lost for words, because the man he wants to meet is hiding at the end of that very corridor. Behind the wooden door, Win hears Juk say he wants to meet "the uncle", and understands that this means him; as the footsteps come closer, his attention lands on the housekeeper's things hanging beside him, and something occurs to him. At the lift, Juk is about to press the call button when Aoy grabs his sleeve and pulls him back, and he turns round smiling: this is the moment he was waiting for.
