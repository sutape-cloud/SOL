GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Seven shots, 24 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: small and natural. Feelings show quietly. The only light is the downlights, the brass wall lamps, the warm hidden lights along the edge of the ceiling, and the morning sun coming through the glass front wall of the lobby. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain. The lift door and its panel are plain brushed steel with only a plain call button. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. Each shot keeps one fixed focal length. Whenever Aoy smiles, it is a closed-lip smile. The only sound is the real sound of the place.

LIP SYNC
Juk speaks in Shots 1 and 2; Aoy speaks in Shots 3 and 7. Each line is lip-synced to the person saying it. In Shot 2 the end of Juk's line goes on while the picture shows his back. The voice files play in order, each exactly once: @Audio1 (Juk) from 0.4 s, @Audio2 (Aoy) from 10.0 s, @Audio3 (Aoy) from 19.6 s. A voice file may end with a stretch of silence that is there only to make the file long enough; only the spoken words are used, and the acting carries straight on to the next beat. Everything ends within 24 seconds.

BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. Juk walked into the lobby to go up and meet Aoy's sick "uncle"; Aoy followed him into the corridor to the lift and grabbed his sleeve just as he reached for the call button, and he turned round to her smiling, sure he had caught her lying. Behind the wooden door at the far end of the corridor, in the housekeeper's rest room, Win, who is the "uncle", turned his light-green jacket inside out so that it is now white with green trim, and put on the housekeeper's pink knitted hat with the big cream flower.

SCENE
Juk is sure there is no uncle, and he gives Aoy one last chance to confess. Then the uncle walks out of the corridor behind him: Win, in a white jacket and a pink flowered hat, standing very still and trying to look like a sick old man. Aoy is so stunned she says "uncle" out loud before she can think. Juk turns round, and for once he has no plan for what he sees. Aoy snaps straight into the part of the bossy niece: she runs to her "uncle", takes his arm, scolds him for wandering off on his own, and walks him out into the lobby, while Juk backs out after them.

AOY'S FACE
Her skin tone is natural, as in her character references. First the shock empties her completely; then, in a moment, she is the bossy niece.
