GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Four shots, 20 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: small, simple and unhurried. Feelings show quietly. Aoy stays dry-eyed. The only light is the low late-afternoon sun through the tall glass walls, and the downlights in the ceiling. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. The camera keeps one fixed focal length per shot. The only sound is the real sound of the lobby.

LIP SYNC
Juk speaks in Shot 1 and Shot 3. The old woman speaks in Shot 2 and Shot 4. Each line is lip-synced to the person saying it. The voice files play in order, each exactly once: @Audio1 in Shot 1, @Audio2 in Shot 2, @Audio3 in Shot 3, @Audio4 in Shot 4. A voice file may end with a stretch of silence that is there only to make the file long enough; only the spoken words are used, and the acting carries straight on to the next beat. Everything ends within 20 seconds.
