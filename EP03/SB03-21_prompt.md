GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Nine shots, 28 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: small and natural. Feelings show quietly. The only light is the morning sun coming through the glass front wall, the warm hidden lights along the edge of the ceiling, the downlights, and the brass wall lamps. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain, the car included: its grille and wheel centres are plain and its number plates are blank. The lift shows only a plain call button. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. Each shot keeps one fixed focal length. Whenever Aoy smiles, it is a closed-lip smile. The only sound is the real sound of the place.

LIP SYNC
Win speaks in Shot 1 and Shot 6; Aoy speaks in Shot 5. Each line is lip-synced to the person saying it. The voice files play in order, each exactly once: @Audio1 (Win) from 0.4 s, @Audio2 (Aoy) from 12.8 s, @Audio3 (Win) from 14.7 s. A voice file may end with a stretch of silence that is there only to make the file long enough; only the spoken words are used, and the acting carries straight on to the next beat. Everything ends within 28 seconds.

BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. In the lobby of her building this morning, Aoy told Win about the huge fine in her job contract. Win photographed every page and said a lawyer friend would go over it; when Aoy guessed he meant Nuch, he made up a story that Nuch studied law before going into stage acting, and Aoy believed him. They sit side by side on the sofa, Aoy at the inner end and Win at the end nearest the glass door, the closed contract folder on Win's knee.

SCENE
Win gently suggests that Aoy skip work today and wait for Nuch to find a way out. She is nodding when a long white car glides past the glass and stops right at the door: Juk has come to pick her up for work. In a flash of panic she grabs Win's arm and tells him to hide. Win is lost in this building he hardly knows. Aoy pulls herself together and walks out to meet Juk at the door, leaving Win alone; Win runs into the corridor to the lift, the lift is too slow, and he slips through the wooden door at the end of the corridor and pulls it almost shut behind him.

AOY'S FACE
Her skin tone is natural, as in her character references. Her shock and how fast she pulls herself together show in her look and her shoulders.
