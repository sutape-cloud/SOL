GLOBAL STYLE

BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. Juk did not believe Aoy's "uncle" was real. Win, who is the "uncle", turned up in his jacket turned inside out, white with green trim, and the housekeeper's pink knitted hat with the big cream flower, and Aoy told Juk with a straight face that her uncle has Alzheimer's. Win, standing right beside her, only found out at that moment what illness he has, and slowly turned toward her. The three of them stand in the lobby in front of the mouth of the corridor to the lift: Juk on the left, in front of the wall with the gold painting; Win in the middle; Aoy on the right, close beside Win, her arm through his.

SCENE
Juk is almost convinced, so Aoy proves it: she turns to her "uncle" and asks him slowly, like a nurse with a patient, whether he remembers her. Her look tells him to play along. Win, who cannot act, nods with a broad, normal smile, then catches her look and freezes halfway through the nod. Aoy asks again what her name is, her look ordering him to stop nodding. He nods again in exactly the same way, completely blank, and this time he really looks like a man who has lost his memory. Juk is convinced. Aoy presents her "uncle" as the proof that he must be looked after, and Juk gives up: fine, bring him along. As soon as Juk walks away to the glass door, the act drops: Aoy holds back a laugh, Win gives her a small real smile, and she straightens the flower on his hat before leading him out.
