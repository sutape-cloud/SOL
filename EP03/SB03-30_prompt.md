GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Nine shots, 26 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: real people, completely serious. The comedy comes from the timing and the silences: five very serious people moving together, far more seriously than one contract needs. Every face stays businesslike. The light is the soft late-morning daylight through the half-open vertical blinds, the warm cove lights, the downlights and the soft white light of the TV; every face keeps its natural skin tone. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain: the book covers, the file covers, the laptop lids, the iPad case and the walls are plain. The big TV shows only an even, plain white glow, edge to edge. The laptop screens show only soft white light. The iPad screen always faces Nuch; the camera sees only the plain black back of its case. Inside the meeting room there are exactly five people: Nuch and the four lawyers. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. Each shot keeps one fixed focal length. The only sound is the real sound of the place.

SCENE
Nuch gives the order like a general before a battle: find a way for the contractor to end this contract within its own terms, as fast as possible. The lawyers study the contract on the TV as one. Anon dares to ask whether the contractor is one of the company's own people. Nuch makes them wait, then gives only this: someone very important to the company, in the future. The four lawyers pass the question around the table with their eyes, stay silent, and throw themselves into the work together. Out in the corridor, an office worker passing the glass sees how seriously the room is taking it and hurries away.
