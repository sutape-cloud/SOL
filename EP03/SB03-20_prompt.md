GLOBAL STYLE
Live-action microdrama, 16:9. Real people, real places, natural skin. Seven shots, 24 seconds, at real-time speed.
All the acting is natural and real, at the size of real life: small and natural. Feelings show quietly. The only light is the morning sun coming through the glass front wall, the warm hidden lights along the edge of the ceiling, and the downlights. The air is clear and clean. Every surface is plain. Every person has two arms and two legs, natural and correct. Each shot keeps one fixed focal length. Whenever Aoy smiles, it is a closed-lip smile. The only sound is the real sound of the place.

LIP SYNC
Win speaks in Shot 4 and Shot 6; Aoy speaks in Shot 5 and Shot 7. Each line is lip-synced to the person saying it. The voice files play in order, each exactly once: @Audio1 (Win) from 10.0 s, @Audio2 (Aoy) from 12.7 s, @Audio3 (Win) from 16.9 s, @Audio4 (Aoy) from 20.7 s. A voice file may end with a stretch of silence that is there only to make the file long enough; only the spoken words are used, and the acting carries straight on to the next beat. Everything ends within 24 seconds.

BEFORE THIS VIDEO
All of this has already happened before the first frame. In the lobby of her building this morning, Aoy has just told Win that the job contract she signed says that if she refuses what they tell her, she has to pay a fine bigger than the hospital bill. Win has taken the closed contract folder and holds it on his knee. They sit side by side on the sofa, Aoy at the inner end and Win at the end nearest the glass door.

SCENE
Win could say something comforting, but he chooses to act instead: he reads the contract page by page and photographs every page with his phone, then tells her plainly that he will have a lawyer friend go over it. Aoy, curious and half doubtful, guesses he means Nuch, because she saw Nuch reel off the law yesterday. Win, caught off guard, quickly makes up a story that Nuch studied law before she went into stage acting. Aoy believes him and is sincerely impressed, and Win quietly breathes out in relief.

AOY'S FACE
Her skin tone is natural, as in her character references. Her curiosity and doubt show in a small frown and the tilt of her head.
